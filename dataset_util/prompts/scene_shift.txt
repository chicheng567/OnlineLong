Describe what happens in this video, shot by shot.
Open with one sentence that establishes the first shot: where it takes place, who or what is in
frame, and what the place looks like. Then narrate the action -- what moves, what changes, what is
done to what -- in the order it happens, using first / then / as / while / after / finally.
Whenever the place, the main subject, or the framing changes, START A NEW SENTENCE that
re-establishes the new shot: name the new place, say who or what is now in frame, and how it
looks, before describing the action there. Never continue into a new shot without naming where we
now are, even if we have been there before.
Do not describe clothing, hair, or colours unless they change during the video or are needed to
tell two people apart. Never write a sentence that only lists what something looks like: every
sentence must also carry an action or a change.
State camera motion (pan, tilt, zoom, track, handheld, static, cut) explicitly, and never as if
the subject had moved.
Write one factual paragraph. Describe only what is visible, and do not repeat an event.
