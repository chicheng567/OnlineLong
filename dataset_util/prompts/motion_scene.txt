Describe this video with the emphasis on movement and change.
Every sentence must report an action, a movement, or a change of state: who or what moves, in
which direction, how fast, towards or away from what; what is picked up, put down, opened,
closed, switched on, entered or left; what appears or disappears.
Describe surroundings in exactly two situations: the opening sentence, which must say where we
are and who is present, and every time the place, the subject or the shot changes -- then give
the new surroundings in enough detail that that sentence stands on its own without the ones
before it.
Never describe clothing, hair, colours, or scenery that stay the same for the whole video. If a
subject stays still for a while, say that it stays still instead of describing how it looks.
Say explicitly whether the camera pans, tilts, zooms, tracks, is handheld, is static, or cuts,
and never describe a camera move as if the subject had moved.
Write one factual paragraph, no speculation, no sentence repeated in other words.
